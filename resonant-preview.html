§ 02 — How it works

The whole thing takes about as long as pouring a coffee.

I

It joins the meeting.

Connect your Zoom, Meet, or Teams once. Resonant shows up on time, every time — no extension installs for your guests, no awkward bot in the corner.

II

It listens, the way you would.

Our model doesn't just transcribe — it follows the thread. What was decided, what got parked, who's on the hook, and what's still open.

III

It writes the note.

Two minutes after the meeting ends, you get a short, readable summary in your inbox. Decisions, action items, and the three paragraphs that matter. Nothing more.

§ 03 — The details

Built for the people who read their notes twice.

Feature 01

A summary you can read
in under a minute.

Most note-takers dump a transcript and call it a day. Resonant writes two short paragraphs that tell you what the meeting was actually about — the kind of summary you'd send a colleague who missed it.

"The team chose the faster integration path despite the higher maintenance cost, with a plan to revisit in Q3. Dan owns the rollout."
Feature 02

Speaker-aware, actually.

Resonant learns your team's voices over a few meetings. After that, attribution just works — no tagging, no cleanup.

Feature 03

Follow-ups, assigned.

Every action item gets an owner and a due date, pushed to Linear, Asana, or Notion the moment the meeting ends.

Feature 04

Search the conversation.

Ask "when did we agree to move pricing?" and jump straight to the moment it was decided. No scrubbing.

Feature 05

Private by default.

End-to-end encrypted. Your audio is deleted within 24 hours. We don't train on your data. Full stop.

§ 06 — FAQ

Questions, before you ask.

What meeting platforms does Resonant work with?

Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams out of the box. We also have a desktop recorder for in-person meetings or platforms we don't natively support yet.

Do my teammates have to install anything?

No. Resonant joins as a regular meeting participant via calendar invite. Your guests won't need to install an extension, sign up, or do anything differently.

How accurate is the transcription, really?

For standard meetings in clear audio, we consistently hit 94–97% word accuracy. Accents, technical jargon, and overlapping speech still cause hiccups — we don't pretend otherwise.

What happens to my audio?

Audio is encrypted in transit and at rest, processed for transcription, and then deleted within 24 hours. The text summary stays in your workspace. Your data is never used for model training.

Can I edit the notes afterwards?

Yes. Every summary is fully editable. If you make corrections, Resonant learns from them — your notes get more accurate to your team's style over time.

Is there a free trial?

The Solo plan is free forever for up to 5 meetings a month. Team plans include a 30-day free trial with no credit card required.
